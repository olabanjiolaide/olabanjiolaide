Learning under practical constraints.

I study multimodal and federated learning in edge networks, where data quality, privacy requirements, and available resources shape what a system can learn.

Research at the Network Intelligence and Innovation Lab (NI2Lab), Concordia University.

Discuss research & collaboration

The problem

Distributed clients can hold different kinds of data, contribute information of uneven quality, and have different resources available for learning. Privacy protection adds another constraint. These conditions make model quality and system behaviour difficult to assess separately.

Data quality · Client heterogeneity · Limited resources

My approach

I investigate how multimodal fusion, federated learning, and resource allocation interact. My focus is on the trade-offs between the information a model uses, the protection applied to updates, and the resources required to participate in learning.

Multimodal fusion · Distributed learning · Privacy–utility trade-offs

Why it matters

A useful edge-learning system needs to be assessed beyond prediction quality alone. Studying privacy and resource constraints alongside learning helps identify when an approach is practical, where it struggles, and which trade-offs need further work.

Model evaluation · System behaviour · Practical limits
